WHY FGM COMPLICATIONS INTENSIFY (OR EVEN CAN CAUSE) PGAD

Because cutting cannot fully excise internal erectile tissue (such as the 7–10cm crura and vestibular bulbs) or deep pudendal nerve roots, the physical architecture for arousal remains active, just with more signals of pain and discomfort.

Furthermore, severed surface nerves often form painful clusters called neuromas trapped inside post-cutting scar tissue. When these trapped nerve endings fire continuously, they can induce intense burning pressure, hyper-sensitivity, and persistent unwanted arousal—heavily worsening or triggering PGAD symptoms.
